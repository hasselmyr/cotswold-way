Emergency closure near Wortley — check before booking

National Trails posted an emergency closure order for a section near Wortley on 3 September 2026, after drought and high winds. Wortley is at mile 28, between Wotton-under-Edge and Hawkesbury Upton, and it falls on every version of this walk. The notice says a diversion will be in place and that details are to follow; as of the last check nothing further had been published.

It is marked in amber on the map and on the profile, along with the two standing diversions at North Nibley and Beacon Lane Plantation. The Wortley band is approximate, because the extent has not been given. Ring the trail team on 07841 663607 or email cotswoldway@cotswolds-nl.org.uk.

Checked and ruled out

  • Woodside Field, Old Sodbury — motorhomes only, no tents, in their own words. Still listed as a campsite on several sites.
  • Conygres, Wotton-under-Edge — Scout site, exclusive whole-site hire only, no single pitches, no showers.
  • The Cider Orchard, North Nibley — a festival field that opens one week a year.
  • Cotswold Meadow, Chipping Sodbury — permanently closed, still listed by three booking platforms.
  • Hill Farm, Cold Ashton — no camping, and fully booked until 1 November.

Calls to make first

  • Trail team, about Wortley. 07841 663607. The diversion may add distance, and it falls on every version of this walk.
  • The Compass Inn, if you pick 2 or 3 days. 01454 218242. Both shorter versions depend on it taking tents; there is no other pitch between Horton and Lansdown.
  • Hunts Court — four pitches for how many of you? 01453 544632. The tightest bottleneck on the route.
  • Lansdown Campsite — is the tent field open on your dates? 07534 894999. Only matters for the four-day version.
  • Horton — Starvale or Hideaway. 07786 962913 / 07808 912788. Confirm they are trading before planning a day that ends there.
